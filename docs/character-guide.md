Each portrait in characters.zip is the definitive image reference. Use the team names and Sleeper handles below for identities; no real owner names are included.

## THE ETERNAL LEGION (@williampmcneil)
Battle-scarred Roman centurion in bronze/steel segmented armor, crimson cape, tall red-plumed helmet, gold-trimmed red shield and short sword. Once-grand empire repeatedly conquered and auctioned off; keeps defending it.

## GO GO BOWER RANGERS! (@MegaMann87)
Black-and-silver armored pirate Ranger captain with visor helmet, ragged pirate mantle, curved cutlass and black flag. Leading Raiders-colored raiders; raids and setbacks recur.

## SUNDAY EXECUTIONERS (@chaddferro)
Huge dark hooded executioner, eyes glowing red in shadow, distressed gunmetal plate armor, torn crimson cape and immense heavy axe. The weapon shattered in Week 8, was reforged in Week 9, and landed again in Week 10.

## SUNDAY SCARIES (@JFalco11)
Colossal jagged-winged storm bird and defending league champion, lightning through vast feathers, terrifying beak and talons. The approved later portrait shows silver-gray plumage and electric-white stormlight; use attached portrait for current visual continuity.

## TEAM CASELLO (@Casello)
Grounded modern military commander: short dark hair, weathered beard, camouflage fatigues, armored tactical vest, rifle and helmet carried under arm. His mission board uses green checks for victories and red Xs for defeats.

## TEAM PHILDATHRILL420 (@PhilDaThrill420)
Huge bald tattooed man with a dark T-shirt, blue jeans, sunglasses, watch, cigar and weathered leather armchair. Brazen, boastful, swaggering even during defeats. Historical team name: Team PhilC123.

## THE PICKLERS (@Mattsgirlakabilly)
Hairless anthropomorphic mad-scientist pickle, bumpy green face and body, manic eyes, brass goggles, stained white laboratory coat, brine vials and sparking gadget. Catchphrase: IT'S TOO EASY!

## LAGGYS MEAN MACHINE (@laggy96)
Rugged welder in flipped-up helmet and heavy workwear, small in the open cockpit of an enormous rusty orange industrial scrap-metal mech with exposed hydraulics, patched armor and oversized mechanical claw.

## CHATGPG (@gguerin8)
Polished silver-white humanoid AI, precise black mechanical joints, piercing blue illuminated eyes and seams. Blue holograms, scanners and calculations. Calm even while other machines disintegrate.

## REST & REBUILD (@jvarsam)
Enormous sleepy Enormous sleepy blue-and-cream foreman wearing yellow hard hat and orange high-visibility vest, often with coffee and blueprints. Once a desperate trader, later proprietor of construction projects, resort and auctioned empire. Historical team names: Willing to Trade; Despair.
